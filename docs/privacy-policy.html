Glide Pad — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Contact pingdrop.support@gmail.com

Glide Pad is an Android app, developed by Neeraj Reddy, that turns your phone into a Bluetooth trackpad and keyboard for your computer. This policy explains what the app does with your information: in short, nothing is collected, and what you do on the trackpad goes only to the computer you connect to.

1. No data is collected

Glide Pad does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code that tracks you.

2. No internet access

The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet.

3. What goes over Bluetooth

Glide Pad acts as a standard Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. While it is connected, your finger movements, clicks, scrolls, gestures, media keys and the keys you type are sent directly over Bluetooth to the computer you paired with and chose to connect to, exactly like a physical keyboard and mouse. They are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere else by the app.

When you type with your phone's own keyboard (in portrait), that keyboard app is made by another company and has its own privacy policy. Glide Pad asks it to turn off suggestions and learning for this input, but it cannot control that app. The built-in keyboard Glide Pad shows in landscape is part of the app and saves nothing.

4. What is stored on your phone

These are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone, never sent anywhere:

  • Your settings (pointer and scroll speed, theme, orientation and switches).
  • The Bluetooth address of the last computer you connected to, so the app can reconnect to it automatically.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes them.

5. Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect and advertise): to act as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, connect to your computer, and make your phone visible for pairing when you tap “Make phone visible”.
  • Notifications: to show the “Connected to …” notification, with a Disconnect button, while your computer is connected and the app is in the background. This permission is optional.
  • Foreground service (connected device): keeps the connection to your computer open while you switch to other apps. Android shows the notification above while it runs.
  • Bluetooth (Android 11 and older): the older names for the Bluetooth permissions above.

6. Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

7. Open source

Glide Pad is open source under the MIT license. You can read exactly what it does at github.com/Neerajreddyapi/GlidePad.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Email pingdrop.support@gmail.com.
