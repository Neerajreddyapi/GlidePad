# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Glide Pad to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **pingdrop.support@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
